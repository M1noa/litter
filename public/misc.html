privacy policy

last updated: june 2024


at litter, we value your privacy and aim to be fully transparent about how this service operates:


storage: all files are stored securely on telegram's servers, not our own.

data collection: we do not collect, require, or store any personal information.

accessibility: any file you upload is public and can be accessed by anyone with the link.

analytics: we use anonymous analytics.

third parties: your data is never sold or shared with third parties.


by using our service, you agree to this privacy policy.
